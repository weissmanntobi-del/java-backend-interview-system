# Errata

This page records confirmed corrections that affect the released product.

## Java Backend Interview System v1.0

No public errata have been recorded yet.

## Reporting a correction

Use the content-correction issue form and include:

- product version;
- exact filename;
- page or section;
- current wording or behaviour, paraphrased when necessary;
- proposed correction;
- technical evidence or an authoritative source.

Do not attach or reproduce complete paid pages.

## Correction states

- **Reported:** received but not yet verified.
- **Confirmed:** reproduced and accepted.
- **Scheduled:** assigned to an upcoming release.
- **Released:** corrected in a published version.
- **Declined:** not reproducible, out of scope, or not technically justified.
