# Frequently asked questions

## Is the complete book available in this repository?

No. This is the public companion repository. The full PDFs, SQL laboratory, commercial diagrams, and companion-project archive are distributed through the product page.

## Who is the system designed for?

Java backend engineers with approximately two to five years of experience who need structured preparation across coding, Java, Spring Boot, security, SQL, architecture, production debugging, and mock interviews.

## Must I read all 1,049 pages?

No. Start with the diagnostic and select the matching seven-day, fourteen-day, or thirty-day path. The large question bank is intended as a topic-based reference.

## Does the product contain leaked interview questions?

No. It is an independent preparation system and should not be used to publish employer-confidential or proprietary interview material.

## Are the SQL scripts safe for production?

No. The laboratory intentionally contains slow queries, redundant indexes, unsafe transaction patterns, and large seed operations. Run it only in a local or disposable training database.

## Is a job offer guaranteed?

No. The system is designed to improve preparation quality and interview performance. Hiring outcomes depend on many factors outside the product.

## Where should I report a correction?

Check [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md), then use the content-correction issue form. Do not paste paid pages into the issue.
